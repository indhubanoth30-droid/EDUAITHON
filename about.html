About the Initiative

Learn more about the core organizations, our vision, and the values driving EduAIthon 2026.

About IUCEE

The Indo Universal Collaboration for Engineering Education (IUCEE), established in 2007, is a global initiative dedicated to enhancing the quality and relevance of **engineering education**. By connecting educators, students, researchers, and industry leaders, IUCEE fosters innovation, collaboration, and transformative practices that prepare engineers for a rapidly evolving world. Their core focus is on faculty development, curriculum modernization, and student experiential learning.

About IASF

The IUCEE Annual Student Forum (IASF) is a flagship **student-centered platform** conducted alongside IUCEE’s international conference. It brings together students from diverse institutions to exchange ideas, collaborate on projects, and develop innovative solutions to real-world challenges. The forum promotes leadership, creativity, and global exposure through interdisciplinary, hands-on learning. IASF is designed to be a crucible for next-generation engineering talent.

EduAIthon 2026 Vision

Building on last year’s success, EduAIthon 2026 will be held as part of IASF under the theme: **“Co-Creating Sustainable, AI-Powered Solutions for Engineering Education.”** This year emphasizes sustainability, inclusivity, and long-term impact, inviting students to design adaptive, learner-centered solutions using AI, AR/VR, digital twins, and heutagogical practices. The core vision is to harness cutting-edge technology to build educational tools that are not only effective but also accessible and environmentally conscious, fostering responsible technological innovation in education.

Recap of EduAIthon 2025

EduAIthon 2025, hosted by RV College of Engineering, SPARK, and CEDLR in collaboration with IUCEE, followed the theme: **“Breaking Barriers – Simplifying Complex Engineering Concepts!”** Over 48 hours, 62 teams comprising around 300 participants explored AI, AR/VR, and immersive simulations to design innovative educational prototypes. The event created a vibrant platform for students to showcase creativity, teamwork, and leadership, resulting in several projects that were later integrated into academic research tracks.

Target Audience & Collaboration

The EduAIthon welcomes all **undergraduate and postgraduate students** passionate about engineering, computer science, and education. We strongly encourage **multi-disciplinary teams** that include students from arts, design, and business, as true EdTech innovation requires a blend of technical expertise and human-centered design. Faculty members and industry experts participate as mentors and judges, offering critical feedback, domain expertise, and a pathway for turning prototypes into market-ready solutions. This collaborative environment ensures solutions are both technologically sound and commercially viable.

Expected Outcomes & IP Generation

The hackathon is designed to be an incubator for real-world solutions. Expected outcomes include **fully functional prototypes**, initial business/impact models, and documented **Intellectual Property (IP)** submissions. IUCEE provides resources and mentorship to encourage teams to pursue patent applications or spin off their projects into future EdTech startups post-event. Our goal is for every project to have a tangible path toward real-world implementation.

The Heutagogical Approach

Heutagogy, or **self-determined learning**, is central to the EduAIthon process. Unlike traditional pedagogy, this approach encourages participants to define their own learning objectives and pathways within the challenge constraints. It is about **learning how to learn**, fostering resilience, adaptability, and true ownership over the problem-solving process—skills vital for future engineers and innovators. This means teams operate with a high degree of autonomy, making decisions about technologies, scope, and direction, guided by mentors rather than strict instructions.

Our Core Mission & Values

EduAIthon is more than just a hackathon; it is a commitment to the future of learning built on these guiding principles:

  • Innovation: Driving new ideas and technological advancements in EdTech.
  • Sustainability: Ensuring solutions are long-lasting and environmentally responsible.
  • Inclusivity: Creating educational tools that are accessible to all learners globally.
  • Collaboration: Fostering teamwork across academic disciplines and global boundaries.
